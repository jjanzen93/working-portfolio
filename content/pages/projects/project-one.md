Introduction

In July 2025, Nick Lafredo and I participated in the 30 Days of Summer Jam, with the theme of "high score", and what resulted from that effort is a fun and exciting collection of microgames a la the *WarioWare* series presented with an early-internet style that evokes the fond memory of Flash games. Feel free to take a look at this `<a href="/document.pdf" target="_blank">design document</a>` to get an idea of our process and a reflection on what we learned along the way!

In July 2025, Nick Lafredo and I participated in the 30 Days of Summer Jam, with the theme of "high score", and what resulted from that effort is a fun and exciting collection of microgames a la the *WarioWare* series with an early-internet style that evokes fond memories of Flash games. Finding a balance between a cohesive overall experience with the purposefully disjointed nature of the different mechanics present in each microgame was a really interesting challenge. Luckily, we came out the other end with a product that walks that line well. You can take a look at the development process,and a reflection/postmortem in this design document, and play the game here.
